THE PROBLEM

Online compilers need internet you don't have

Rural and tier-3 college labs often have unreliable or no internet. Students waste exam time configuring compilers instead of writing code.

THE FIX

Everything runs on the machine, offline

Offline Coder bundles real compilers inside the app itself. Copy it to any lab PC and it just works — no network, no admin password, no setup.
